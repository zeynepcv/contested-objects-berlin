Berlin's museums display some of the most famous objects in the world, yet many of them were taken from their places of origin through colonial expeditions, unequal agreements between empires, or outright looting. This small exhibition gathers ten such objects and asks the questions that museum labels often leave out: where did they come from, how did they reach Berlin, and who wants them back?
