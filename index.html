Contested Objects
Introduction Map Objects Project notes

Berlin's museums display some of the most famous objects in the world, yet many of them were taken from their places of origin through colonial expeditions, unequal agreements between empires, or outright looting.

This digital exhibition follows ten such objects, from the Ishtar Gate and the Pergamon Altar to the bust of Nefertiti, the Benin Bronzes and the statue of Ngonnso. It tells each object's biography: where it was made and what it meant there, how it reached Berlin, what happened to it since, and who wants it back.
